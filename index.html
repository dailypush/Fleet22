About Fleet 22 Lake Erie

Fleet 22 Lake Erie features a vibrant community of J/105 sailors dedicated to one-design racing excellence. Our fleet comprises boats from multiple Lake Erie ports including Cleveland, Ohio; Buffalo, New York; and Toledo, Ohio. We foster competitive racing, camaraderie, and a shared passion for sailing on the Great Lakes.
